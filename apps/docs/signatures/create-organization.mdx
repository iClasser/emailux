An **organization** holds your signatures, connections, people and billing. Create one before anything else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to EmailUX">
    Go to [emailux.com](https://www.emailux.com/signup) and sign in, or create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the organization">
    Open **Signatures**. If you have no organization yet, choose **Create an organization** and enter your company name. The company name becomes the built-in *Organization name* field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a logo">
    Upload your logo in the organization settings. You can also upload banners and badges under **Settings, Assets**, so every signature uses the same files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite teammates">
    Open the organization's **People** page and send invites with a role. An invite is a link tied to the invited person's email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Signature Admin** | Manages the organization, people, signatures and billing. |
| **Signature Editor** | Creates and edits signatures. Cannot invite people, change organization settings or manage billing. |
| **Signature Finance** | Manages the subscription, invoices and payment method. |

<Tip>
  A common split is marketing as Editor, who owns the design, and IT as Admin, who owns the Google connection.
</Tip>
