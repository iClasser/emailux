EmailUX reads people from your Google Workspace directory and writes signatures to Gmail. Setup takes a **Google Workspace Super Admin** and uses domain-wide delegation, so employees never click through a consent screen.

<Note>
  Individuals can also connect their own Google account to publish to their own mailbox. Use the domain-wide setup to cover the whole domain.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    In EmailUX, open **Signatures, Directory** and go to the **Connection** section. It walks through three stages: **Connect**, **Authorize** and **Sync**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Start the connection with the Google account of a Super Admin. EmailUX uses it to identify your Workspace customer and domains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize domain-wide delegation">
    In the Google Admin console, open [Domain-wide delegation](https://admin.google.com/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation) and add the client ID EmailUX shows you with the scopes below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync the directory">
    Return to EmailUX and choose **Sync now**. People, groups and organizational units appear under **People** and **Groups**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scopes to authorize

Paste these when Google asks for OAuth scopes. They are read-only for the directory, plus Gmail settings for signatures. There is no Drive scope.

```text
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.userschema.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.member.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.orgunit.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.domain.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.customer.readonly
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.sharing
```

## After connecting

- **People** lists directory users and their fields.
- **Groups** lists directory groups you can use in rules.
- **Fields** shows the profile fields available to your signatures.
- **Rules** assigns signatures. See [assignment rules](/signatures/assignment-rules).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Google could not connect">
    Confirm you are signed in as a Super Admin, that delegation lists every scope above, and then retry. Scope changes can take a few minutes to apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The connection was cancelled">
    You closed the Google consent screen. Start the connection again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="People are missing after sync">
    Only active users with a ready mailbox receive signatures. Check that the user is active in Google Admin and run **Sync now** again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
