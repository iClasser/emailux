Publishing renders one signature per person and writes it to that person's Gmail settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the prerequisites">
    You need a signature, a connected Google Workspace directory, and at least one rule that assigns the signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    On **Signatures, Directory**, choose **Sync now**. EmailUX refreshes the directory, applies your rules and queues each mailbox that changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in Gmail">
    Open a test user's Gmail, choose **Settings, See all settings, General, Signature**, or compose a new message and confirm the signature appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the queue works

- Each mailbox has its own queue entry with a **pending** or **failed** state once it has been written.
- If a signature has not changed, the mailbox is **skipped**.
- A mailbox that fails is marked **failed** and the error is attached. EmailUX retries it later.
- Only active users with a ready mailbox are published.

## When people change

Update the directory, then sync. A new title, department or phone number flows into the next render. Moving a person out of a rule or adding them to an **Ignore** rule stops the publishing for that person.

<Warning>
  EmailUX writes the signature to Gmail's signature setting. Gmail decides where it is applied, so mail sent from other clients may not include it. Test the clients your people use.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A mailbox shows failed">
    Read the attached error. The most common causes are a missing delegation scope, a suspended user or a deleted mailbox. Fix it, then sync again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing is published">
    Confirm that a rule assigns a signature and that the connection is active. People matching no rule receive nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fields appear empty">
    Check the person's directory entry and the [field order](/signatures/data-sources). Add a fallback or custom field if the directory lacks the value.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
