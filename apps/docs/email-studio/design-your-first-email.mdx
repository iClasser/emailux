<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email Studio">
    Sign in at [emailux.com](https://www.emailux.com) and open **Email Studio**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a template">
    Start a new email and name it by purpose, for example `welcome-email`. The name becomes your reference when you [send through the API](/email-studio/send-with-the-api).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add blocks">
    Drag in blocks such as header, text, image, button and footer. Drop saved blocks from your **library** to reuse brand pieces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variables">
    Insert variables like `firstName` where content changes per recipient. You supply the values when you send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the code preview">
    Open the code preview to see the exact HTML. It uses table-based layout and inline styles for broad client support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send yourself a sample">
    Send a test to your own inbox and check it in Gmail, Outlook and on mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Move the template from **draft** to **approved**, then **live** once reviewed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer code? The same components are open source. See [Setup](/getting-started/setup).
</Tip>
