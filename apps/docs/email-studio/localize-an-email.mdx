Each template has **one layout** and a **separate set of strings per language**, so you never maintain copies of the design.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the language menu">
    In the editor, open the language selector for your template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a language">
    Choose a language. EmailUX copies strings from a language you pick so you can translate them in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate the strings">
    Edit the text for the new language. Use the AI assist if you want a first draft, then review it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview each language">
    Switch languages in the preview. Arabic and Hebrew switch the template to **right-to-left** automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send with a locale">
    Pass a `locale` such as `fr-FR` when you render or deliver through the [API](/email-studio/send-with-the-api).
  </Step>
</Steps>
