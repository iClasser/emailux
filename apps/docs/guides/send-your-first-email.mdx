This path takes you from an empty account to an email in an inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Design the template">
    Follow [Design your first email](/email-studio/design-your-first-email). Name it `welcome-email`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your provider">
    EmailUX is not the sender of record. Connect [SendGrid](/providers/sendgrid), [Gmail](/providers/gmail) or [SMTP](/providers/smtp-imap) so your own domain and reputation are used, then [create an API key](/providers/api-keys).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your domain">
    Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC with your provider before sending at volume. See the [email authentication guide](https://www.emailux.com/guides/email-authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send from code">
    Use `deliver()` from [@emailux/api-client](/api/api-client) with `channel_data` for the sender and recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Confirm a `200` response and the message in the recipient inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>
